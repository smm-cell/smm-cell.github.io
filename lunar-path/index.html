Lunar Path

Privacy Policy — effective 2026-10-03

1. Introduction

Welcome to Lunar Path! Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how AKFORT LLC (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and protects information in connection with your use of Lunar Path (“the App”).

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of data when you use the App:

  • Device Information (e.g., device model, operating system, device identifiers)
  • App and device integrity signals — when the App checks that it is genuine, Google Play services send Google information about the App (package name, version, signing certificate and Google Play licence status) and a device attestation (whether the device is a genuine, certified Android device, the status of Google Play Protect, and whether apps that can capture the screen or control the device are running). See the Google Play Integrity section.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App
  • Diagnose technical issues
  • Ensure security and prevent fraud or abuse
  • Verify that the App is genuine, installed from Google Play and running on a genuine device, to protect players against cheating, tampering and fraud

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain collected data only as long as necessary for the purposes described above, and it is automatically deleted within 90 days. You may also request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us using the details below, and we will process your request promptly.

5. Advertising

Lunar Path does not display advertising and does not share your data with advertising networks for ad targeting.

6. Google Play Integrity

To protect Lunar Path and its players from tampering, piracy and fraud, the App uses the Google Play Integrity API (Google LLC). When the App runs this check, Google Play services send the information described in the Information We Collect section to Google, which returns a signed verdict about the App and the device. According to Google, this data is encrypted, is not transferred to any third parties and is deleted after a fixed retention period. We use the verdict only to verify that the App and the device are genuine; it is not used for advertising and is not sold. Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy and the Play Integrity API terms.

7. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for analytics and crash reporting (for example, to measure app performance and fix bugs). These providers process data in accordance with their own privacy policies. We do not sell your personal data.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at smm@akfortllc.sbs.
